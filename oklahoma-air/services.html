Heating & air services

Repair, replacement and complete installations for homes and businesses across the Oklahoma City metro.

Service and repair

Our factory-trained technicians diagnose, adjust and repair heating and air conditioning systems, and service calls are flat-rate priced, so you know the cost before we start.

We advise against servicing your own unit. It isn't safe, and it can void your manufacturer's warranty.

Equipment replacement

Choosing a new system is a big decision. We help you choose the comfort system that gives you the best value for your home, and we install only Trane equipment.

Complete system installation

Our comfort consultants assess your home first, then design and install a system suited to how your family lives.
