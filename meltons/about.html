Three generations of service in Pryor

Melton's has kept Mayes County cool, warm and running for more than 70 years.

How it started

  1. 1951
    Leroy Melton opens the shop

    Melton's Sales and Refrigeration starts selling and repairing air conditioning and appliances, and installs the first refrigerated air unit in Pryor, at the Spot Cafe.

  2. Next
    The sons join in

    Roger and Randy Melton graduate from Pryor High School, attend OSU Tech, and go to work alongside their father.

  3. 1985
    A company of their own

    Roger and Randy buy the heating, air and refrigeration side of the business and set up their own company.

  4. 2017
    Remembering Roger

    Roger was the face of Melton's for decades. "Putting customers first" was his top priority, and it still guides us.

  5. Today
    Still growing

    The company has grown every year since it began, serving homes and businesses across northeast Oklahoma.

How we work

Our technicians keep their training current through classes and seminars. They're dispatched by cell phone, and every van carries diagnostic equipment. Our office staff is trained in the trade, and we keep a fully stocked parts department, so more repairs get finished on the first visit.

Our goal

To be the most effectively operated HVAC, refrigeration and appliance repair company in northeast Oklahoma.
