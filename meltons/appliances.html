Quick fixes to try first

Fridge not cooling?

Check the temperature dial, clean the condenser coils and fan, and wipe down the door gaskets.

Dryer taking forever?

Clean the lint trap, look for kinked flex tubing and clear lint from the exhaust vent. Avoid white vinyl vent tubing, which is a fire risk.

Dishes coming out dirty?

Check the spray arms and spray holes for clogs and clear debris around the drain. A hot cycle with a cup of white vinegar can help.

Washer banging on spin?

Adjust the four feet to level the machine, or set it on a piece of carpet if the floor is uneven.

Appliance acting up?

Call the office and we'll get a technician headed your way.
